Self-healing relay network

Or click any relay on the map to destroy / restore it.
How it heals · rules running in every node
  1. HELLO beacon every 30 s: “I am N hops from the gateway”.
  2. Neighbour table: a neighbour silent for 3 beacons (90 s) is dropped.
  3. Gradient: my hops = best live neighbour + 1. The network rebuilds its own map.
  4. Closer-only forwarding: hand the SOS to a neighbour with fewer hops; it must ACK.
  5. No ACK in 2 s: retry the next-best neighbour. Routes change without any central control.
  6. Store and carry: no route at all → keep the SOS, forward it the moment a route returns. Nothing is dropped.
